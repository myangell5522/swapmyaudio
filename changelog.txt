0.2.2

- Title-screen Volume page: the Audio Device line moves down when The End Of A Calamity Menu Theme (or another mod) adds a volume slider, and no longer runs into the Back button.
- New Mod.Call API (Supported, Label, Value, Cycle) for mods with their own settings menus.

0.2.1

- Sound no longer leaks back to the old device for a moment when the new one falls behind.
- Delay on the new device stays low in long sessions instead of slowly growing.
- Unplugging the selected device switches the menu back to System Default.
- If switching fails, the menu keeps showing the device that's actually playing.
- The Volume menu checks for new devices less often, so it does less work every frame.
